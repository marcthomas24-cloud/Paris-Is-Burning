Touch: drag on the left of the screen to move, drag on the right to look. STRIKE, SHIELD (hold), DODGE and SPRINT (toggle) are bottom right; tap the on-screen prompt to arrest or respond.

The baton locks on to the nearest aggressor in the direction you are looking or moving; one right on top of you is hit whichever way you face.

Threats are determined by behaviour, never appearance. Aggressors show a red ring when acting violently.
