PARIS BURNING · LOGO SAMPLES

Six directions in the tricolore (bleu #0055A4 · blanc · rouge #EF4135). All are live: CSS/SVG/canvas, no image files, so any of them can go straight onto the menu.

A

MOLTEN TRICOLORE

The letters are liquefying: thick, glossy drips crawl down off every letter, swell and fall away. Slow, heavy and unsettling, with a blood-red glow beneath.

B

WREATHED IN FLAME

Real-time fire rises off the tops of the letters and licks across the front of them. The tricolore letters are scorched black at the base and heat-glowing at the top.

C

CHARRED FLAG

The tricolore as a flag being burnt: holes eaten through the letters with glowing, flickering ember edges and soot creeping out around them. Embers drift up.

D

RIOT GRAFFITI

Sprayed in tricolore on a concrete wall: rough edges, overspray and paint runs that slowly creep down. Lit from below by an unseen fire that flickers across the wall.

E

SMOULDERING

Burnt to charcoal, with the tricolore still alive inside: white-hot cracks in blue, white and red that breathe and pulse like embers. The most restrained and the most sinister.

F

TRICOLORE INFERNO

The fire itself burns in the flag's colours, blue at the root, then white, then red at the tips, around letters burnt to black silhouettes.
